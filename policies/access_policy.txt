COMPANY ACCESS CONTROL POLICY

Policy Number: AC-2024-002
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

WORKING HOURS:
- Standard working hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- After-hours access requires manager notification
- Weekend access requires prior approval

FILE ACCESS NORMS BY ROLE:

ENGINEER:
- Normal files per day: 10-20
- Sensitive files per day: 2-5
- Expected work hours: 9 AM - 6 PM

MANAGER:
- Normal files per day: 8-15
- Sensitive files per day: 3-8
- Expected work hours: 9 AM - 7 PM

ANALYST:
- Normal files per day: 15-25
- Sensitive files per day: 5-10
- Expected work hours: 8 AM - 5 PM

INTERN:
- Normal files per day: 5-10
- Sensitive files per day: 0-2
- Expected work hours: 10 AM - 4 PM

SUSPICIOUS BEHAVIOR INDICATORS:
1. Login outside working hours without notification
2. File access volume >3x normal
3. Accessing Restricted data without authorization
4. Downloading data to USB drives or personal cloud
