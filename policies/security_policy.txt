COMPANY SECURITY INCIDENT RESPONSE

Policy Number: SEC-2024-003
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

INSIDER THREAT RESPONSE PROTOCOL:

RISK LEVEL 1 (0-9%) - NORMAL:
- No action required
- Continue monitoring

RISK LEVEL 2 (10-29%) - LOW:
- Log incident for pattern analysis
- Review after 7 days if pattern continues

RISK LEVEL 3 (30-49%) - MEDIUM:
- Notify direct manager
- Monitor for 24 hours
- Schedule awareness training if needed

RISK LEVEL 4 (50-69%) - HIGH:
- Immediate notification to Security Team
- Temporary restriction of access
- Investigation within 2 hours
- Employee interview within 24 hours

RISK LEVEL 5 (70-100%) - CRITICAL:
- Immediate account suspension
- Physical security alerted
- Forensic investigation initiated
- Legal department notified
- Potential termination process

ESCALATION MATRIX:
- Security Analyst → Initial review (15 min)
- Security Lead → Escalation (1 hour)
- CISO → Critical incidents (immediate)
- Legal → Data breach potential (2 hours)
